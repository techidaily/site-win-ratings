## Infographic Guide: How to Choose the Ultimate Karaoke Track - A DJ's Perspective

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/whatosing-coverimagejpg.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/whatosing-coverimagejpg.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "whatosing-coverimagejpg")

Pro KJs using our [karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)will get surely a big kick out of this infographic from [BuzzFeed](http://www.buzzfeed.com/h2/fbsp/motorolamotox/this-infographic-will-perfectly-choose-your-ideal-karaoke-so). I could see many printing it out and hanging in the KJ Booth.

It begins straight forward enough, asking _“What Should You Sing For Karaoke Tonight?”_ Then branches out into many humorous selections, such as _“Do you want to Air Guitar?”_ and _“Are you trying to impress someone?”_. Amazingly, they’ve managed to narrow the song selection down to only 16 songs, that cover all your karaoke singer’s requirements. Brilliant job, and if you have karaoke singers that ask you _“What can I sing that’s good?”_ you have a contingency plan already in place — _check the infographic!_
